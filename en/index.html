Apartments

1, 2 and 3 rooms

Efficient layouts, with no space wasted on corridors. Every apartment has a balcony, natural light in every room and direct access outdoors from the living area. Usable areas start at 37 m² for one room and reach 81 m² for three rooms, while ground-floor apartments have a private garden. The basement storage room and the underground parking space can be purchased separately.

Finishes

Premium, included in the price

The apartment is handed over fully finished. A warm palette in sand tones, light oak flooring and natural stone in the bathrooms — materials chosen for durability, not just for show. Windows are thermally insulated, bathrooms have underfloor heating and ambient lighting is built into the design. There is no additional fit-out stage and no unexpected costs after handover.

Living area

Open-plan living, dining and kitchen

The kitchen opens onto the living room and the dining area receives direct light. The balcony extends the living space instead of closing it off — especially useful when you have guests. Generous distances between buildings and the GF+3 height mean light reaches the room all day long, not just for a few hours in the morning.

Entrance and storage

Generous hallway, storage room included

A hallway with room for a full-height wardrobe and a private storage room in the basement. Seasonal items have their place and the home stays uncluttered. Building access is by intercom, every staircase has a lift, and the car stays in the basement — not in front of your window.

09 — Questions

Frequently asked questions

For further questions, the sales team replies on the same working day.

Where is Emerald City?
In Iași, Păcurari area, with access from Strada Ion Nistor. About 4.7 km to the city centre and 4.3 km to Copou.
How many apartments does the development have?
925 apartments with 1, 2 and 3 rooms, in 18 buildings, built in three phases: 322 in Phase I, 423 in Phase II and 180 in Phase III.
What sizes are the apartments?
Between 37 and 81 m² of usable area, with a balcony in every apartment. Ground-floor units have a private garden, between 13 and 51 m².
What does GF+3 mean?
Ground floor plus three storeys. All 18 buildings have the same height, with a maximum of 18 metres. Below them are two basement levels, with parking and storage rooms.
How many parking spaces are there?
940 in total: 258 underground and 682 at surface level. The underground space is purchased separately.
Are the finishes included in the price?
Yes. The apartment is handed over fully finished, with materials selected by a design studio. There is no additional fit-out stage.
How is payment made?
A 15% deposit when the pre-sale agreement is signed and the balance on handover, from own funds or through a bank loan. Interim payments are optional.
Can I buy with a mortgage?
Yes. Every apartment page has an indicative mortgage calculator. The documentation required by the bank is provided by the developer.
Is there an agency commission?
No. Sales are made directly by the developer, Tala Sapphire S.R.L.
Can I visit the construction site?
Yes, by appointment. We also publish the construction progress monthly, with dated photos, in the Site diary section.
