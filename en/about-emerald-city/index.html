01 — The vision

Low density, room to live

Building height and density determine the quality of living in the long run.

The development spreads horizontally, not vertically. The 18 buildings have a ground floor and three storeys, with a maximum height of 18 metres. The consequences are measurable: natural light even on the ground floor, few apartments per staircase, light lift usage and generous distances between buildings.

Of the 50,235 m² of land, 15,501.80 m² is landscaped green space, i.e. 30.85%. It includes paths, a park, playgrounds and cycle lanes, all part of the master plan. Parking is provided with 940 spaces, 258 of them underground, so that cars do not take over the pedestrian areas.

Apartments are handed over fully finished, with underfloor heating and high-performance windows. Ground-floor units have a private garden and all have a balcony. Sales are direct, with no agency commission.

09 — Check

How to check a developer

The criteria below allow any residential project to be checked before signing a pre-sale agreement.

  • The building permit, with its number and issue date
  • The urban planning certificate and the approved indicators: site coverage and floor area ratio
  • The legal status of the land and the land book extract
  • The technical annex of finishes, item by item
  • The handover date written in the pre-sale agreement, not promised verbally
  • Previously delivered projects that can be visited
  • Who builds the structure and who checks the design

11 — Questions

About the developer, quality and warranties

The 16 questions we answer most often, with concrete figures and deadlines.

Who is developing Emerald City?
Tala Sapphire S.R.L., a Green Stone Group company. Sales are made directly by the developer, with no agency commission.
What experience does the developer have?
Emerald City is developed by Tala Sapphire S.R.L., a Green Stone Group company — a group with projects in the United Kingdom, Israel and Romania. In Iași the group is also developing Lapis and Onyx Residence, and in Sibiu it delivered phase I of Magnolia Residence, with 1,132 apartments.
What warranty do I have for the building's structure?
The load-bearing structure is guaranteed for the whole life of the building, under Law 10/1995 on quality in construction.
How long is the developer liable for hidden defects?
10 years from final acceptance, under Law 10/1995 and the Civil Code. Apparent defects are reported within the first year after handover.
What documents do I receive at handover?
The acceptance report, the building technical book, the cadastral documentation, the land book extract with registration in the buyer's name and the energy performance certificate.
Are the apartments handed over finished?
Yes, fully finished. Flooring and tiles laid, bathrooms equipped, walls skimmed and painted, doors fitted, electrical installation with fittings, underfloor heating. The apartment is ready to furnish on the acceptance date.
What is a condensing boiler?
It recovers heat from the flue gases instead of venting it through the chimney. For the same comfort, it uses up to 35% less gas than a conventional boiler.
What is the advantage of underfloor heating?
Heat spreads evenly from the floor up, with no radiators on the walls, and runs at low temperature, so it uses less energy. Walls also stay free for furniture.
Why buy directly from the developer?
No agency commission is charged, price and deadlines are agreed directly with the developer, the availability list is public and construction progress can be followed monthly.
What deposit is required at the pre-sale agreement?
15% of the price when the pre-sale agreement is signed before the notary, and the balance on handover. Payment stages are set in the contract.
Can I buy with a mortgage?
Yes. Apartments can be bought with a standard mortgage, and those within the programme's ceiling can also be bought through the Noua Casă scheme. The sales team presents the option suited to each budget.
Do prices include VAT?
Yes, the listed prices include VAT.
What amenities does the neighbourhood have?
Cycle lanes, fitness areas, playgrounds, private parking, pre-wiring for EV charging stations, lifts, photovoltaic panels, green spaces and a park, underground waste collection.
How much green space does the development have?
15,501.80 m² of landscaped green space, i.e. 30.85% of the 50,235 m² plot. Site coverage is 30% and the floor area ratio is 1.80.
When will the development be completed?
Construction is split into three phases: 322 apartments in Phase I, 423 in Phase II and 180 in Phase III. Each phase's deadline is confirmed in the contract.
Where is the sales office?
Str. Dealul Zorilor 9, Păcurari area, Iași. Opening hours: Mon–Fri 9–18 and Sat 10–14. Phone 0757 70 70 80.
