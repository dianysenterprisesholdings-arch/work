01Who we are and what the policy covers

The data controller is Tala Sapphire S.R.L., with its registered office at Str. Ion Nistor, Iași, Iași County, Romania, registered with the Trade Register under no. [J22/…/…], tax ID [RO…], part of Green Stone Group (the “Controller”). The policy describes the processing of personal data of visitors to emerald-city.ro, of people who send requests through forms, phone, e-mail or WhatsApp, of showroom visitors and of prospective buyers, up to the signing of a contract.

Processing is carried out in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Law no. 190/2018 and Law no. 506/2004 on the processing of data in the electronic communications sector.

Data protection officer: dpo@emerald-city.ro.

02What data we collect

  • Identification and contact data: first name, last name, phone number, e-mail address.
  • The content of the request: the apartment or category of interest, indicative budget, preferred day and time slot for a viewing, number of people, free-text message.
  • Pre-contractual transaction data (only at the showroom, on reservation): identity document details, home address, intended financing method.
  • Technical data collected automatically: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, traffic source, cookies (detailed in the Cookie policy).
  • Preferences saved locally in the browser: the list of apartments saved for comparison, the display mode of the availability list. These preferences stay on your device and are not transmitted to the Controller.
  • Records of communications: e-mails, WhatsApp messages and consultants' notes on phone or showroom conversations.

We do not collect special categories of data (ethnic origin, political opinions, health, etc.) and do not target minors; the Website is intended for people aged 18 or over.

04How we collect data

Directly from you, through the Website's forms, phone, e-mail, WhatsApp, social media or at the showroom. Automatically, through cookies and similar technologies, when you visit the Website. Occasionally, from third parties: real estate platforms through which you requested information about the project, or people who recommended us with your consent.

05Who we share data with

  • Processors providing us with services: web and e-mail hosting, the customer relationship management (CRM) provider, communication service providers (telephony, WhatsApp Business), the agency managing our promotional campaigns. They process data only on our instructions and under a data processing agreement.
  • Green Stone Group companies, for internal reporting and administration, on the basis of legitimate interest.
  • Notaries public, banks and credit brokers, only at your request, for reservation or financing.
  • Public authorities, courts, legal and accounting advisers, where required by law or to defend our rights.

We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

06Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some services used (for example Google Maps for the map on the Website or the WhatsApp platform) may involve transferring data to providers based outside the EEA. Such transfers are made only to countries with a European Commission adequacy decision or on the basis of standard contractual clauses and appropriate supplementary measures.

08How we protect data

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures: data transmission over encrypted connections (HTTPS), role-based access control, authentication for internal systems, backups, staff training and confidentiality obligations for consultants and suppliers. In the event of a security breach posing a high risk to your rights, we will inform you in accordance with Art. 34 GDPR.

09Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, objection (including to direct marketing) and the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing. Consent may be withdrawn at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. Details and how to exercise these rights are in the GDPR notice.

Requests are sent to dpo@emerald-city.ro and answered within one month at most. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (dataprotection.ro).

10Direct marketing and social media

Commercial communications by e-mail or messages are sent only with prior consent, and every message contains an unsubscribe option. Our pages on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube are also governed by those platforms' policies, with which we may be joint controllers for page statistics.

11Updates

The policy may be updated to reflect legislative or operational changes. The current version, with its update date (25 September 2026), is published on the Website. Significant changes will be clearly flagged.
