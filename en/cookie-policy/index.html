01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by the browser on your device when you visit a website. They allow the device to be recognised on later visits, preferences to be remembered and traffic to be measured. Similar technologies include browser local storage (localStorage), tracking pixels and device identifiers.

This policy is issued by Tala Sapphire S.R.L., with its registered office at Str. Ion Nistor, Iași, Iași County, Romania, registered with the Trade Register under no. [J22/…/…], tax ID [RO…], part of Green Stone Group, and is supplemented by the Privacy policy.

02Legal basis

Cookies strictly necessary for the Website to work are used on the basis of legitimate interest and do not require consent. All other categories (preferences, statistics, marketing) are activated only after consent is given through the cookie banner, in accordance with Art. 4 of Law no. 506/2004 and the GDPR. Consent may be withdrawn at any time, with effect for the future.

04Third-party content

The map in the “Showroom” section is provided by Google Maps. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies and process your IP address in accordance with its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Links to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and WhatsApp open those platforms, which apply their own rules.

05How to manage cookies

  • Through the consent banner shown on your first visit, where you can accept or refuse each category and change your choice at any time via the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.
  • From your browser settings: Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge allow cookies and local storage to be blocked or deleted. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect some sections (for example, the comparison list).
  • Through the opt-out tools of statistics and advertising providers, when they are active, and through youronlinechoices.eu for behavioural advertising.

06Data collected and rights

Data obtained through cookies is handled in accordance with the Privacy policy. The data subject's rights (access, erasure, objection, etc.) are described in the GDPR notice and are exercised via dpo@emerald-city.ro.

The policy is reviewed whenever the tools used change. Last updated: 25 September 2026.
