01Identity and contact details of the controller

This notice is provided under Art. 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and is addressed to Website visitors, people who contact us, showroom visitors and prospective buyers.

02Purposes and legal basis of processing

  • Replying to requests, booking and holding viewings — pre-contractual steps at your request (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Sending personalised offers, plans and payment terms — pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Reserving a unit and concluding the deeds — performance of the contract and legal, tax and notarial obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)).
  • Availability notifications and commercial communications — consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Website security, aggregated statistics, defending legal claims — legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Video surveillance at the showroom, where applicable — legitimate interest in the safety of people and property, with signage at the entrance.

03Categories of data and their source

Identification and contact data, the content of requests, purchase preferences, pre-contractual data (on reservation), technical browsing data and records of communications. The data comes directly from you or, in limited cases, from real estate platforms through which you requested information or from people who recommended you with your consent.

04Recipients of the data

Processors (hosting, e-mail, CRM, communications, promotion agency), Green Stone Group companies, notaries, banks and credit brokers at your request, public authorities and professional advisers where required by law. Details in the Privacy policy.

05Transfers to third countries

Certain providers (Google, Meta/WhatsApp) may process data outside the European Economic Area, exclusively on the basis of adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission, with supplementary safeguards.

06Retention period

Requests without a reservation: 24 months from the last interaction. Notification subscriptions: until consent is withdrawn. Contractual data: the term of the contract plus 10 years. Technical logs: a maximum of 12 months. Cookies: according to the Cookie policy.

07Rights of the data subject

  • The right of access (Art. 15): confirmation of processing and a copy of the data.
  • The right to rectification (Art. 16): correction of inaccurate data or completion of incomplete data.
  • The right to erasure (Art. 17): in the cases provided by law, for example when the data is no longer needed or consent has been withdrawn.
  • The right to restriction of processing (Art. 18).
  • The right to data portability (Art. 20): receiving the data in a structured, commonly used format, for processing based on consent or contract.
  • The right to object (Art. 21), including, at any time and without justification, to direct marketing.
  • The right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing (Art. 22). We do not use such decisions.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority.

08How to exercise your rights

Send a request to dpo@emerald-city.ro or in writing to the controller's registered office, stating the right invoked and the necessary identification details. We reply within one month of receipt at most; the period may be extended by two months in complex cases, with notice to you. Exercising your rights is free of charge, except for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

To protect the data, we may request additional information to verify your identity before replying.

09Supervisory authority

The National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru no. 28-30, district 1, Bucharest, postal code 010336; phone +40 318 059 211; e-mail anspdcp@dataprotection.ro; www.dataprotection.ro.

10Whether providing data is required

Providing contact data is necessary to reply to requests and to book a viewing; without it, the request cannot be processed. Providing data for notifications and commercial communications is voluntary. Full identification data is required by law to conclude reservation and sale deeds.

11Updating this notice

This notice is updated whenever the purposes, recipients or retention periods change. Last updated: 25 September 2026.
