01 — The group

Green Stone Group, “Delivering Home”

A group of companies dedicated to real estate development and urban regeneration, active in three markets.

Green Stone Group develops projects that combine contemporary design with ecological solutions. The portfolio covers housing, commercial facilities and public spaces, with a focus on revitalising degraded or under-used urban areas — instead of expanding the city onto open land.

The group operates in the United Kingdom, Israel and Romania, which means construction and finishing standards come from more demanding markets than the local one. In Romania it delivered phase I of Magnolia Residence in Sibiu, with 1,132 apartments, and is developing Lapis and Onyx Residence in Iași.

Emerald City is the group's third project in Iași and its largest: 925 apartments on five hectares, developed through Tala Sapphire S.R.L.

08 — Questions

About the group and portfolio

8 questions about who is building and what has been delivered so far.

Who is developing Emerald City?
Emerald City is developed by Tala Sapphire S.R.L., a Green Stone Group company. Sales are direct, with no agency commission.
What is Green Stone Group?
A group of companies dedicated to real estate development and urban regeneration internationally, with projects in the United Kingdom, Israel and Romania.
What other projects does the group have in Iași?
Lapis Residence, started at the end of 2023, and Onyx Residence, its extension, started in 2024. Together, the two developments total 740 apartments.
Has the group delivered completed projects?
Yes. Phase I of Magnolia Residence in Sibiu, with 1,132 apartments, two complexes in the United Kingdom and several developments in Israel, one of them completed in 2016.
What does urban regeneration mean?
Turning degraded or under-used parts of the city into liveable places, with housing, commercial facilities and public spaces — instead of expanding the city onto farmland.
Why does it matter who the developer is?
Warranties, deadlines and build quality depend on the company signing the contract and on the projects it has delivered before. A developer with completed developments that can be visited can be verified.
Can I visit the previous projects?
Yes. The Iași developments can be visited; addresses and access details are provided by the sales office.
What warranties do I get at Emerald City?
The load-bearing structure is guaranteed for the whole life of the building and hidden defects for 10 years from acceptance, under Law 10/1995 and the Civil Code. Full details are on the “About us” page.
