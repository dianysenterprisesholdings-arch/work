04 — Arguments

Why Iași and why Păcurari

Four factors supporting long-term rental demand.

Steady rental demand

The country's second-largest university city and a growing IT hub. Demand for studios and 2-room apartments does not depend on a single employer.

4.8 km from the centre, 5 km from Copou

Direct public transport to the universities and the city centre. Păcurari is among the first choices of tenants who work or study in north-western Iași.

New building, low running costs

A thermal envelope to current standards, an own boiler and individual metering: predictable monthly costs and rare repairs in the first years.

Direct from the developer

No agency commission on purchase and a listed price for every unit. The saving goes straight into the yield.

06 — Information

What to take into account

The elements that separate an estimate from the actual result.

Gross yield and net yield

Gross yield divides the annual rent by the purchase price. Net yield deducts vacancy periods, income tax, management costs and repairs. The difference between the two is usually at least one percentage point.

Costs not in the calculator

Notary fees, land registration, the applicable VAT, initial furnishing and equipment and any management fee are not included. For a studio, full furnishing generally starts at a few thousand euros.

The informative nature of the figures

The values on this page are estimates based on listing prices and rents observed in the area. They are not financial or tax advice; the purchase decision belongs to the buyer, who may consult an independent specialist.

The advantages of a new home6 items
  • Structural and finishing warranties, as required by law
  • Own boiler and individual metering
  • Low consumption: thermal envelope and triple glazing
  • Turnkey handover: the tenant can move in after furnishing
  • Underground parking and storage room, on request
  • Complete documentation: permits and land registration
07 — Questions

About investment and yield

8 questions about rents, costs, demand and calculation method.

What gross yield do Emerald City apartments have?
At listing prices and the rents observed in the Păcurari area, the estimated gross yield is between about 4.5% and 5.5% per year, highest for studios. The figures are estimates, not guaranteed values.
How is the monthly rent estimated?
Rent is calculated at about €6.2/m² of usable area per month, an average observed for new, turnkey-finished homes in the Păcurari area. The actual value depends on furnishing, floor, orientation and the time of letting.
What is the difference between gross and net yield?
Gross yield divides the annual rent by the purchase price. Net yield deducts vacancy periods, income tax, management costs and repairs. The calculator applies a cost of about 8% and a configurable vacancy rate.
Which costs are not in the calculator?
Notary fees, land registration, the applicable VAT, initial furnishing and equipment, and any property management fee. For a studio, full furnishing generally starts at a few thousand euros.
Are the apartments handed over finished, ready to let?
Yes. Handover is turnkey: flooring, tiles, sanitary ware, own boiler, underfloor heating, interior doors and triple-glazed windows. Only furniture and appliances remain to be added.
Can several units be bought in the same building?
Yes. For multiple purchases, the sales team can prepare a selection of units on the same landing or in the same building, with a dedicated commercial proposal, at the showroom.
What rental demand is there in the Păcurari area?
Iași is the country's second-largest university city, with over 60,000 students, plus employees in IT and services. Păcurari is 4.8 km from the centre and about 5 km from Copou, on a direct public transport corridor.
Is the information on this page investment advice?
No. The figures are estimates based on listing prices and rents observed in the area and are for information only. The purchase decision belongs to the buyer, who may consult an independent financial or tax specialist.
