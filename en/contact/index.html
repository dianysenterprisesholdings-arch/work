04 — Questions

About contact and opening hours

6 questions about opening hours, response time and viewings.

What are the sales office's opening hours?
Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 and Saturday from 10:00 to 14:00, at the showroom on Str. Dealul Zorilor 9, Păcurari area, Iași. Outside these hours, requests received by e-mail or WhatsApp are picked up on the next working day.
How quickly will I get a reply?
Requests sent through the form or by e-mail are answered on the same working day if sent before 16:00. Phone calls are answered directly during opening hours.
Are viewings by appointment only?
Yes. The show apartment and the site are visited by appointment, so that a consultant is available exclusively for the meeting. Appointments can be made online, by phone or on WhatsApp.
Can plans and price lists be requested by e-mail?
Yes. The full availability list, the plans of each layout and the payment terms are sent by e-mail or WhatsApp, on request.
Is there parking at the showroom?
Yes, free parking in front of the sales office on Str. Dealul Zorilor. The area is also served by public transport, with a stop about 300 m away.
Who answers the requests?
The developer's sales team, Tala Sapphire S.R.L., part of Green Stone Group. No intermediaries are involved and no agency commissions are charged.
