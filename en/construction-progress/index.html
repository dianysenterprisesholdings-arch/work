07 — Questions

About construction and deadlines

6 questions about following progress and contractual guarantees.

How often is the page updated?
Monthly. The status of each phase is published stage by stage, together with dated site photos. Last update: September 2026.
What do the percentages mean?
Each phase is split into 7 construction stages, from earthworks to external landscaping. A phase's overall percentage is the weighted average of its stages, using the usual weights for apartment buildings.
Can the site be visited?
Yes, by appointment and accompanied by a representative, wearing the protective equipment provided. Unaccompanied access is not allowed, for safety reasons.
Are the handover dates guaranteed?
Each phase's deadline is written into the pre-sale agreement signed before the notary. The dates shown here are estimates as of the last update; the contractual deadline is the binding one.
What happens if the works are delayed?
The pre-sale agreement sets the handover deadline and the consequences of exceeding it. Any change to the schedule is communicated in advance.
Can the monthly report be received by e-mail?
Yes. On request, the monthly report with photos is sent by e-mail as soon as it is published.
