Project

18 buildings, not 4 towers

The same built area could come from a few towers or from 18 four-storey buildings. The second option uses more land, but decisively changes natural light, noise levels and the distances between façades.

01 — Concept

Why four storeys

Building height is set once, at permitting, and determines the quality of living for the whole life of the development.

A development of 925 apartments can be built as four towers or as 18 buildings of 2B+GF+3. The first option uses less land and costs less. The second was chosen.

The consequences are measurable. Few apartments per staircase limit the load on lifts and common areas. The low building height reduces mutual shading, so natural light reaches even the ground floor. Distances between buildings exceed the regulatory minimum.

The two basement levels take the parking and storage, so the space between buildings is kept for paths, the park and playgrounds. 123 of the ground-floor apartments have a private garden, between 13 and 51 m².

09 — Questions

About the project and indicators

10 questions about how the development is designed, with figures from the documentation.

How tall are the buildings?
2B+GF+3: two basement levels, ground floor and three storeys. The maximum parapet height is 18.00 metres, the same for all 18 buildings.
What is on the two basement levels?
The first basement level houses the underground car park, with 258 spaces and lift access straight to the staircase. The second holds the storage rooms and technical spaces.
What do site coverage 30% and floor area ratio 1.80 mean?
Site coverage is the share of the plot that can be built on at ground level: 30% of the 50,235 m², i.e. about 15,070 m². The floor area ratio is the total floor area divided by the plot area; 1.80 is the value approved for this site.
How much green space does the development have?
15,501.80 m², i.e. 30.85% of the plot — more than the built footprint.
In how many phases is it built?
Three: 6 buildings and 322 apartments in Phase I, 8 buildings and 423 apartments in Phase II, 4 buildings and 180 apartments in Phase III.
How many parking spaces are there?
940 in total: 258 underground, on the first basement level, and 682 at surface level within the development.
What commercial units are planned?
Two, on the ground floor of buildings 6 and 12: 722.22 m² and 125.82 m², 848.04 m² in total.
Who designed the development?
Design 266/2023, building permit stage, was prepared by S.C. C.A.D. S.R.L. of Iași, with arch. Ovidiu M. Murgu as lead architect, for the client Tala Sapphire S.R.L.
Can I see the permitting documentation?
Yes. The building permit, the urban planning certificate and the drawings can be consulted at the sales office, on request.
Does every building have a lift?
Yes. All 18 buildings have a lift that goes down to the first basement level, where the underground car park is, so access from the car park to the staircase is direct.
