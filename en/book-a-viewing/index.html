03 — Questions

About viewings

8 questions about length, hours and what can be seen.

How long does a viewing take?
About 40 minutes: a presentation of the show apartment, the plans and the availability list, followed by questions. On request, the meeting continues with a site visit.
What can be seen on site?
The fully finished show apartment, the finish samples, the development's site plan and, with protective equipment, the buildings under construction.
Can a viewing be booked on a Saturday?
Yes, on Saturdays between 10:00 and 14:00. Saturday slots fill up faster, so we recommend booking a few days ahead.
Is the appointment confirmed?
Yes. After the form is sent, a consultant confirms the day and time by phone on the same working day. The appointment can be changed or cancelled at any time, by phone or WhatsApp.
Is any document or preparation needed?
No. It only helps to have an idea of your budget and the number of rooms you want, so the presentation can focus on the right units.
Can several people attend?
Yes. Family, a financial adviser or an architect are welcome. Mention the number of people in the form so the meeting can be organised.
Can an apartment be reserved on the day of the viewing?
Yes. A reservation locks in the listed price for the chosen unit until the pre-sale agreement is signed, under the terms communicated at the meeting.
Does a viewing involve any obligation?
No. The viewing is free and involves no obligation to buy.
