07 — Questions

About 2-room apartments

8 questions about areas, layouts, features and terms.

What is the difference between types 2A and 2B?
2B has a larger bedroom and extra storage space, with the same living area configuration. Areas differ by a few square metres.
How big is a 2-room apartment?
Between 51 and 61 m² of usable area, depending on the layout. All have a balcony.
Which ones have a private garden?
Those on the ground floor. Gardens are between 13 and 51 m² and for exclusive use.
Is it suitable for a family with one child?
Yes. The separate bedroom and open living area meet the needs of a young family, and the development has a playground and landscaped green spaces.
What is a storage room?
A private space on the second basement level, for luggage, bicycles and seasonal items. It is contracted separately, subject to availability in the building.
What is the deposit at the pre-sale agreement?
15% of the price, when the pre-sale agreement is signed before the notary. The balance is paid on handover or according to the agreed schedule.
When are the 2-room apartments handed over?
Depending on the phase the building is in. The handover date is written into the pre-sale agreement for each unit.
Is a parking space included?
The parking space is contracted separately, underground or at surface level. The development has 940 spaces, 258 of them underground.
