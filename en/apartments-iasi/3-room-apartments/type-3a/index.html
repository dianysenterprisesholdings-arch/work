07 — Questions

About 3-room apartments

8 questions about areas, layouts, features and terms.

How big is a 3-room apartment?
Between 69 and 81 m² of usable area, depending on the layout. All have a balcony.
What is the difference between types 3A and 3B?
3A has a closed kitchen and two bedrooms. 3B has an open-plan living room, one bedroom and a third room that can be used as a study. Both have two bathrooms.
Do they have two bathrooms?
Yes, both three-room layouts include two fully finished and equipped bathrooms.
Are there 3-room apartments with a garden?
Yes, those on the ground floor. Gardens are between 13 and 51 m², for exclusive use.
How many parking spaces can I contract?
The number of spaces that can be contracted per apartment is agreed at the sales office, depending on availability in that building.
Can rooms be merged or changed?
Non-structural changes are reviewed at the sales office, depending on the building's construction stage. Load-bearing walls are not changed.
What orientation do the 3-room apartments have?
It varies from unit to unit; each apartment's orientation is shown in the availability list and on its page.
How big is the balcony?
The balconies of the three-room apartments are the most generous in the development. The exact area is shown on each unit's page.
