06 — Questions

About 1-room apartments

8 questions about areas, layouts, features and terms.

How big is a studio?
Between 37 and 39 m² of usable area, with a balcony. Ground-floor units also have a private garden.
Are they suitable as an investment?
Yes. Studios have the shortest time to let on the Iași rental market, and the investment page includes a yield and payback calculator.
Do they qualify for the Noua Casă scheme?
Starting prices are below the scheme's ceiling. Exact eligibility is checked at the time of purchase, together with the bank.
What layout do they have?
Type 1A: an open living area with the kitchen integrated into the living room, a bathroom and storage space.
Are there studios with a private garden?
Yes, those on the ground floor. The garden is for exclusive use, with outdoor flooring laid and its own socket.
Can it be bought with a mortgage?
Yes, with a standard mortgage or, within the ceiling, through the Noua Casă scheme. The minimum deposit at the pre-sale agreement is 15%.
What are the running costs of a studio?
Utilities are metered individually, and underfloor heating with a condensing boiler keeps costs low. Service charges are set by the owners' association.
What rent can be achieved?
The estimate depends on the floor, the contracted features and the time of letting. The investment page includes a calculator based on each unit's price and the rents observed in the area.
