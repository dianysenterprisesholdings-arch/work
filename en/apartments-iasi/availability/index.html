03 — Questions

About the list and prices

6 questions about updates, statuses and how to read the list.

How often is the availability list updated?
The list is generated from the developer's sales ledger and is updated at every change of status: reservation, signing of a pre-sale agreement or release of a unit.
What do the statuses Available, Reserved and Sold mean?
Available: the unit can be reserved. Reserved: it is held in a buyer's name until the pre-sale agreement is signed. Sold: the pre-sale agreement or the contract has been signed.
Are the prices in the list final?
The listed prices include VAT and all finishes. They become firm on reservation or when the pre-sale agreement is signed; until then they may be updated together with the list.
What does the price per square metre represent?
The unit's price divided by its usable area. The balcony, garden, storage room and parking space are not part of the usable area, which is why units with a garden show an apparently higher price per square metre.
How do I filter by phase or by layout?
The Phase I–III and Type 1A–3B buttons combine with the other filters. Every selection is reflected in the page address, which can be shared or saved.
Can the full list be received by e-mail?
Yes. The availability list, with areas, floors, orientations and prices, is sent by e-mail on the same working day, on request.
