09 — Questions

About the apartments and buying

10 questions about areas, prices and how to buy.

How many apartments are available now?
The number shown on this page is updated from the sales ledger. The full list, with filters by rooms, budget, floor and building, is in the availability section.
What sizes are the apartments?
Between 37 and 81 m² of usable area. Every apartment has a balcony, and ground-floor units have a private garden, between 13 and 51 m².
Do the listed prices include VAT?
Yes. Prices on the site include VAT and all finishes. There are no extra costs for their installation.
What does starting price mean?
The lowest price among the available apartments in that category, as of the last update. Each unit's price is shown individually in the list.
Can I buy with a mortgage?
Yes. Apartments can be bought with a standard mortgage, and those within the ceiling can also be bought through the Noua Casă scheme.
What deposit is required?
15% of the price when the pre-sale agreement is signed before the notary, and the balance on handover. Payment stages are set in the contract.
Is there an agency commission?
No. Sales are made directly by the developer, with no agency commission.
Can I reserve an apartment?
Yes. The apartment is held in the buyer's name and the price is maintained for the reservation period. The term is agreed at the sales office.
Which apartments are handed over first?
Those in Phase I, buildings 1–6. Each phase's deadline is written into the pre-sale agreement.
Can I see an apartment before buying?
Yes. The show apartment can be visited at the sales office and the plans of each layout can be consulted.
