01 — Included in the price

10 advantages, at no extra cost

Each feature, with its technical specification and the concrete benefit in use.

Underfloor heatingIn every room, with an individual manifoldHeat spreads evenly from the floor, with no radiators on the walls. Walls stay free for furniture and consumption drops, as the system runs at low temperature.
Condensing boilerHigh efficiency, room-by-room controlIt recovers heat from the flue gases, extracting more energy from the same cubic metre of gas. The difference on the bill reaches up to 35% compared with a conventional boiler.
External wall insulationContinuous insulation, no thermal bridgesThe façade is insulated along its whole perimeter, including slabs and lintels, which reduces heat loss in winter, heat gain in summer and outside noise.
7-chamber PVC windowsTriple glazing, Low-E glassThe seven-chamber profile and triple glazing insulate against heat and noise to the standard required of new buildings. The Low-E coating reflects heat back into the room.
10 mm laminate flooringHeavy-traffic grade10 mm thick, rated for heavy traffic. Laid on a sound-absorbing underlay, it reduces impact noise transmitted to the floor below.
Fully finished bathroomsFloor and wall tiles, sanitary ware, tapsTiling, toilet, washbasin, taps and connections are installed. No further work is needed after handover.
Complete electrical installationFittings installed, consumer unit with breakersSockets and switches are installed, the consumer unit is equipped and labelled, and circuits are sized separately for the kitchen, bathroom and rooms.
Finished wallsFine skim coat and washable paintWalls and ceilings are skimmed and painted. The surface is ready for immediate furnishing or for another colour, with no extra preparation.
Interior doors fittedMatt finish, hardware includedAll interior doors are fitted, with frames, architraves and hardware. The entrance door is metal, insulated, with multi-point locking.
Video intercomAn indoor station in every apartmentLets you identify visitors before opening. Access to the building and the underground car park is controlled, by card or code.

Exact technical specifications and brands are confirmed in the technical annex of the sale contract.

04 — Questions

About finishes and warranties

8 questions about what is included, what is not and what can be changed.

What does turnkey handover mean?
The apartment is handed over fully finished: floors laid, walls skimmed and painted, bathroom equipped, doors fitted, electrical installation with fittings and working underfloor heating. It is ready to furnish at acceptance.
Are the finishes included in the price?
Yes. The listed price includes VAT and all 10 finishing items. There are no extra costs for their installation.
What is not included?
Furniture, appliances, decorative light fittings and garden landscaping. The connections and circuits for them are, however, prepared.
Can I change the finishes?
Changes are reviewed at the sales office, depending on the building's construction stage. The number of available options decreases as works progress.
What warranty do I have for the finishes?
Finishes are guaranteed under the technical annex of the contract, and installations and equipment under each manufacturer's warranty. The structure and hidden defects are covered separately, by law.
Why underfloor heating and not radiators?
Heat spreads evenly from the floor up and runs at low temperature, so it uses less energy. Walls also stay free for furniture.
What are 7-chamber windows?
The number of air chambers in the window profile. Seven chambers, combined with triple glazing and Low-E glass, insulate against heat and noise to the standard required of new buildings.
How much do I save with the condensing boiler?
Up to 35% on gas consumption compared with a conventional boiler, because it recovers heat from the flue gases instead of venting it through the chimney.
